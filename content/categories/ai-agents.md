---
name: AI Agents
slug: ai-agents
---
AI agents carry out multi-step tasks with limited supervision, often running on cloud machines so work continues while the user is away. Some are marketplaces of task-specific agents; others are single general-purpose assistants. Buyers should examine how usage is metered — typically credits — the per-run cost, concurrency limits, supported integrations, and how predictable monthly spend is under a credit model.
