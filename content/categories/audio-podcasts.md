Audio and podcast tools cover AI text-to-speech, voice cloning, dubbing, transcription, and audio editing. They turn scripts into narration, translate voices across languages, and clean up recordings. Key buying factors include the monthly character or credit quota, how many custom voices are allowed, commercial-use licensing on free versus paid tiers, output audio quality, and the range of supported languages.
