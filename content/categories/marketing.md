Marketing tools help teams attract, track, and convert customers across channels. This category spans call tracking and attribution, conversation intelligence, campaign analytics, and AI-assisted content and outreach. Because most charge by usage — calls, minutes, contacts, or credits — the important comparisons are what each plan includes, the per-unit overage rates, contract flexibility, and which AI analysis features are gated to higher tiers.
