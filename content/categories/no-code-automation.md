No-code and automation tools let people build workflows, scrape data, and connect apps without writing software. They range from visual workflow builders and web-scraping platforms to AI app generators, trading manual, repetitive tasks for reusable automations. Buyers usually weigh execution limits, credit or usage metering, integration coverage, and how well each platform respects the rate limits of the services it automates.
