Resume and cover-letter tools help job seekers tailor applications to specific postings and to the applicant tracking systems that screen them. They score keyword and formatting matches, generate cover letters, and optimize LinkedIn profiles. The comparisons that matter are the number of free scans, whether scans and AI edits are unlimited on paid plans, trial terms, and what happens to saved data after cancellation.
