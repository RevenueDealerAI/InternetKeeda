## What it does
Jobscan is a resume-optimization tool. It measures how closely an application lines up with a chosen posting and the applicant tracking software that screens candidates, then scores keyword and formatting alignment. It also bundles a resume builder, a LinkedIn optimizer, an AI cover-letter generator, a job tracker, and a browser extension.

## Pricing
The Free plan costs $0 and includes 5 resume scans per month plus 5 ATS and recruiter findings. Paid plans, checked 2026-07-30, are the Quarterly plan at $29.98 per month (billed $89.95 every three months) and the Monthly plan at $49.95 per month; both unlock unlimited scans and unlimited AI optimizations. A seven-day free trial is offered on the quarterly plan only.

## Limits that bite
The Free tier is capped at 5 resume scans per month. The seven-day trial applies to the quarterly plan; selecting the monthly plan provides no trial period. On cancellation, access continues until the period ends, after which access to paid features — and the data associated with those features — is lost.

## How it compares
Teal bundles a free resume builder and job tracker with keyword matching. Rezi centers on ATS-optimized resume generation. Enhancv emphasizes design-forward templates over match scoring.

## Good fit / poor fit
A good fit for applicants tailoring resumes to individual postings who want a concrete match score before applying. A poor fit for heavy users on the Free plan, since 5 scans per month is quickly exhausted, or for anyone wanting to trial the cheaper monthly plan first.
