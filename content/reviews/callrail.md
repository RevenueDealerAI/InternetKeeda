## What it does
CallRail is a call-tracking and conversation-intelligence platform for marketers. It attributes phone calls, texts, and form submissions to the campaigns that drove them, and its AI transcribes and analyzes calls to surface leads and keywords.

## Pricing
All four plans include 5 local numbers, 250 local minutes, and 25 texts. Pricing, checked 2026-07-30, runs from $50 per month for Lead Tracking (billed annually), through Lead Tracking Complete at $95 and Lead Conversion at $150, to Lead Conversion Complete at $195. Every account starts with a 14-day free trial and requires no credit card, and plans are month-to-month with no long-term contract.

## Limits that bite
Base plans include only 250 minutes and 5 numbers; beyond that, overages bill at $0.06 per local minute, $3 per extra number, and $0.03 per text, and CallRail states these charges are non-refundable. Minutes are rounded up to the nearest minute before totaling. Premium Conversation Intelligence is limited to the $150-and-up tiers.

## How it compares
By CallRail's own comparison pages, CallTrackingMetrics' comparable plan starts at $179 per month with per-activity AI fees, while Invoca is quote-based and typically requires annual enterprise contracts.

## Good fit / poor fit
A good fit for agencies and local businesses needing call attribution with usage-based flexibility and AI transcription. A poor fit for high call volumes where per-minute overages accumulate, or for teams outside the US, Canada, UK, and Australia.
