## What it does
PhantomBuster is a no-code automation platform for extracting data and running actions across web platforms, with a library of more than 130 pre-built automations ("Phantoms") covering lead generation, scraping, and outreach across networks such as LinkedIn.

## Pricing
The Free plan includes 30 minutes per month of execution time, 1 automation slot, and 50 MB of storage, with exports and API output limited to 10 rows. Paid plans, per PhantomBuster's pricing blog dated 2026-07-21, are Start at $69 per month (20 execution hours, 5 slots), Grow at $159 per month (80 hours, 15 slots), and Scale at $439 per month (300 hours, 50 slots). Annual billing lowers Start to $56 per month. A 14-day trial provides 2 run hours.

## Limits that bite
Execution is metered in hours, so a data-heavy job on Start can consume its 20 monthly hours quickly. Free-plan exports and API output are capped at 10 rows. PhantomBuster's own guidance caps LinkedIn activity for safety at roughly 20 connection requests per day on free or premium accounts.

## How it compares
Apify targets developers with code-first "actors." Expandi focuses on native LinkedIn-plus-email sequences with conditional logic. La Growth Machine charges per identity, starting at €60 per month per seat.

## Good fit / poor fit
A good fit for growth and sales teams automating repetitive social and scraping tasks without code. A poor fit for very high-volume scraping on the entry plan, or for teams uncomfortable operating close to a platform's automation limits.
