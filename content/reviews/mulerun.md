## What it does
MuleRun is an AI agent marketplace where users run pre-built agents to complete tasks and creators publish agents. Agents execute on a dedicated cloud virtual machine that runs continuously, so tasks can continue while the user is offline.

## Pricing
Pricing is credit-based, where $1 equals 100 credits; every account receives 500 bonus credits on sign-up plus 200 bonus credits refreshed daily. Paid tiers, checked 2026-07-30, are Plus at $16 per month (2,000 credits), Super at $32 per month (4,500 credits), and Pro at $160 per month (23,000 credits). Annual billing carries a 20% discount, and credits can be topped up at any time.

## Limits that bite
Because spend is metered in credits and the per-agent run cost is not published, monthly cost is hard to predict before running tasks. The Free tier caps concurrency at 10 tasks and storage at 10 GB. Monthly credits are finite, so heavy use requires top-ups beyond the plan allowance.

## How it compares
Manus, a comparable agent product, is credit-based and priced higher at entry. Genspark offers a daily free-credit allowance but publishes limited paid pricing. Convergence runs a single general agent rather than a creator marketplace.

## Good fit / poor fit
A good fit for users who want to try many task-specific agents without building their own, and for creators seeking distribution. A poor fit for teams needing predictable, seat-based billing, given the opaque per-run credit cost.
